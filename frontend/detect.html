    Treatment advice

      For a clear result

      • Use daylight. Avoid deep shadow and the camera flash.
      • One leaf, close up. Fill most of the frame with the damaged leaf.
      • Hold steady. A sharp photo helps more than a big one.
      • Check a few leaves. Photograph plants from different parts of the field.
