From photo to plan in three steps

Made for the field, not the office

CropGuard is designed for smallholder farmers in Rwanda: quick on a phone, light on mobile data, and clear without technical words.

A helper, not a laboratory. CropGuard gives a computer estimate from a photo and currently supports maize and cassava only. For a severe outbreak, confirm with your local agronomist.

Why we built it

We studied Rwanda's national agricultural household survey. Maize grows on almost every farm, many farmers report smaller harvests than last season, and almost none get advice by phone.
